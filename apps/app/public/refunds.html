StoryStitch refund policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This covers StoryStitch Plus, the one-time purchase that removes the "Made with StoryStitch" mark from exports.

Purchases on the web

  • You can ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase by emailing ashishnag94@gmail.com.
  • Paddle is the merchant of record for web purchases, and it processes the refund.

Purchases in the iOS app

  • Apple sells and refunds purchases made in the iOS app. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

After a refund

Once a purchase is refunded, the "Made with StoryStitch" mark returns to your exports.
