StoryStitch support

Need help, or found a problem? Email ashishnag94@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Do my photos get uploaded?

No. StoryStitch does everything on your device, and your photos and collages are never uploaded. The privacy policy has the details.

Do I need an account?

No. You can make and share collages without one. An account is only needed to save your own layouts.

I didn't get my confirmation or reset email

Check your spam folder, and make sure you used the right address. You can ask for a new password link from Account, then Forgot your password.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account, then Delete account, and enter your password. This permanently removes your account and your saved layouts.
