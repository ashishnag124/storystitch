StoryStitch privacy policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

StoryStitch ("we") is run by Ashish Nag. This policy covers the StoryStitch website and iOS app: what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: ashishnag94@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your photos stay on your device. StoryStitch finds subjects, arranges and crops your photos, and makes your collage on your device. Your photos and collages are never uploaded.
  • You don't need an account to make and share collages. An account is only needed to save your own layouts.
  • No ads, no third-party tracking, and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

What When Why
Email address and a hashed password You create an account To sign you in, confirm your email and reset your password
Saved layouts: the name you give each one and the shape of its cells, with no photos You save a layout To show your layouts in your account
Sign-in session details: a session token, IP address and device or browser type You're signed in To keep you signed in and protect your account
Attempt counts for sign-in, sign-up and password reset You use those forms To limit abuse
Technical logs, which can include IP address and time You use the app or website To run the service and keep it secure. Kept for a limited time by our hosting providers
A running count of how often each built-in layout is shared You share a collage To put popular layouts first. It records the layout only, not who shared it, when or from where

When you're signed in on the website, we use one cookie to keep you signed in. On iOS, your session is kept in your device's secure keychain.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your photos, your collages, what's in your photos, your contacts or your location. Subject detection runs on your device, and its results never leave it.

On iOS, you choose photos in Apple's picker, and StoryStitch only receives the ones you select. If you save a collage to Photos, iOS may ask permission to add it to your library.

Who processes data for us

We use these providers only to run StoryStitch:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website and runs the bot check on sign-up, sign-in and password reset. The bot check receives technical information about your device and connection. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Railway runs our server and database, which hold your account and saved layouts.
  • Resend sends confirmation and password-reset emails, so it receives your email address.

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and saved layouts until you delete them. Sign-in sessions expire on their own. Attempt counts are short-lived. Layout counts are kept because they aren't linked to anyone.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a saved layout at any time from the Layouts tool.
  • Delete your account in the app or on the website, under Account, then Delete account. This permanently removes your email address, password, sessions and saved layouts. Any copies in backups are removed as those backups expire.
  • Get a copy of your data, correct it or ask a question by emailing ashishnag94@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live, such as California, the EU or the UK, you may have further rights, including complaining to a regulator. We honor these requests for everyone.

Children

StoryStitch isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, never as plain text, and connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we'll tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and show a notice in the app.
